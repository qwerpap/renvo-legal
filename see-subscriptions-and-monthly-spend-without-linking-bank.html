How to See Your Subscriptions and Monthly Spend on iPhone Without Linking a Bank

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvo developer

Short answer. Add your subscriptions to Renvo by hand and it totals them per month and per year, converts mixed currencies and shows the trend by month. Nothing is read from your bank and there is no account, so the numbers are only as complete as what you enter.

Step by step

  1. List what you pay forCheck your Apple ID subscriptions, your card statements and your email receipts for recurring charges.
  2. Add each one to RenvoEnter name, price, currency, billing cycle and start date. Weekly, monthly and yearly plans are all supported.
  3. Open the totalsRenvo shows monthly and yearly totals, the average cost and the most expensive plan.
  4. Set the display currencyIf you pay in several currencies, pick one and Renvo converts the totals.
  5. Review the trendCheck the spending trend by month and cut what you do not use.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. Renvo works from subscriptions you add yourself, so nothing is read from your bank.

Is there an account or sign-in?

No. Everything is stored on your iPhone and reminders are local notifications.

How many subscriptions can I add for free?

One. Renvo Pro ($4.99/month or $34.99/year, with a free trial) removes the limit.

Will Renvo cancel a subscription for me?

No. It reminds you in time so you can cancel in Apple ID settings or with the service yourself.

Why not link the bank?

Linking finds charges automatically but means sharing bank access with a third party. Renvo trades that convenience for privacy.

Will I miss subscriptions?

Possibly, because you add them yourself. Checking Apple ID subscriptions and statements once covers most.

Does it show yearly cost?

Yes, both monthly and yearly.
