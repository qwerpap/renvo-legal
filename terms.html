Subscriptions

Renvo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. A free trial, if offered, converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

Your records, not your bank's

Renvo shows the subscriptions you enter. It does not connect to banks or cancel subscriptions on your behalf, and reminder dates follow the dates you enter. Currency conversions use public rates and are approximate.
