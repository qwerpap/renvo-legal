How to Set a Monthly Subscription Budget on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvo developer

Short answer. Add your subscriptions to Renvo, then set a monthly budget for them. The app shows the limit, what your subscriptions use and what is left, with a progress indicator, so you see when you are over before the next bill lands.

Step by step

  1. Add your subscriptionsEnter each subscription with its price and billing cycle so the monthly total is correct.
  2. Set the budgetOpen the budget card and enter the monthly amount you want subscriptions to stay under.
  3. Watch the progressSee the limit, the remaining amount and the progress ring.
  4. Use the calendarCheck which payments fall on which days of the month.
  5. Trim what is too muchCancel or downgrade the plans that push you over the budget.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. Renvo works from subscriptions you add yourself, so nothing is read from your bank.

Is there an account or sign-in?

No. Everything is stored on your iPhone and reminders are local notifications.

How many subscriptions can I add for free?

One. Renvo Pro ($4.99/month or $34.99/year, with a free trial) removes the limit.

Will Renvo cancel a subscription for me?

No. It reminds you in time so you can cancel in Apple ID settings or with the service yourself.

Is the budget only for subscriptions?

Yes. Renvo is not a full expense tracker; it budgets recurring subscriptions.

Does it handle yearly plans?

Yes, yearly plans are converted to a monthly equivalent.

Which currency is the budget in?

Your chosen display currency.
