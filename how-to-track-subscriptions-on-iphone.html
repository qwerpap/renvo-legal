How to Track All Your Subscriptions on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvo developer

Short answer. Install Renvo, add each subscription with its price, billing cycle and start date, and it shows the next charge, a monthly and yearly total and sends a reminder before each renewal. You enter subscriptions yourself, so no bank connection or account is needed.

Step by step

  1. Install Renvo and open itDownload Renvo from the App Store and go through the short onboarding.
  2. Add a subscriptionPick a popular service or type any name, then enter the price, currency, billing cycle (weekly, monthly or yearly) and start date.
  3. Check the next chargesThe Home screen shows which payment is next, how much it is and in how many days, and the next 30 days day by day.
  4. Read your totalsSee what all subscriptions cost per month and per year, and the most expensive plan.
  5. Turn on remindersChoose how many days before each renewal Renvo should notify you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. Renvo works from subscriptions you add yourself, so nothing is read from your bank.

Is there an account or sign-in?

No. Everything is stored on your iPhone and reminders are local notifications.

How many subscriptions can I add for free?

One. Renvo Pro ($4.99/month or $34.99/year, with a free trial) removes the limit.

Will Renvo cancel a subscription for me?

No. It reminds you in time so you can cancel in Apple ID settings or with the service yourself.

Can I see everything on a calendar?

Yes. The payment calendar marks the days payments are due.

What currencies are supported?

18 currencies, with automatic conversion into the one you choose.

Is my data shared?

Subscriptions are stored on your device; Renvo has no account system.
