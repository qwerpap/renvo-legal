Renvo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Renvo is a subscription tracker. We built it to collect nothing about you.

Your subscriptions

The subscriptions you add — names, prices, dates, categories — and your answers in onboarding are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reminders

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Currency rates

To convert prices between currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. The request contains no personal data and no information about your subscriptions.

Purchases

Renvo Pro is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

What we don't do

No accounts, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
