Common questions

I didn't get a reminder. Check Renvo → You → Settings → Reminders, and that notifications for Renvo are allowed in iPhone Settings.

The next charge date is wrong. Open the subscription and set the date you started paying (and the trial length, if any) — the renewal dates follow from it.

How do I restore my subscription? Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel Renvo Pro? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Renvo.
